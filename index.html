SHINGEKI NO KYOJIN · ATTACK ON TITAN

The Greatest Show To Ever Exist

Created by Hajime Isayama, Attack on Titan (Shingeki no Kyojin) is a global anime phenomenon that redefined dark fantasy storytelling. What begins as a desperate struggle for human survival behind towering walls evolves into a multilayered exploration of freedom, morality, and geopolitical intrigue. Blending relentless action with flawless plot twists and unmatched narrative depth, it challenges the very nature of conflict—earning its place as a defining masterpiece of modern television.

The Seasons

Tap a season to reveal what's waiting inside.

Humanity has lived in peace behind three concentric walls for over a century — until a Titan taller than the wall itself shatters that illusion in a single afternoon. Eren, Mikasa, and Armin watch their home fall, and swear an oath that will consume the rest of their lives. This is where the nightmare begins.

The wall itself has secrets, and one of them is standing inside the Survey Corps' own ranks. As a Titan breach inside Wall Rose turns into a hunt for a traitor, the line between friend and monster blurs — and the truth about who the real enemy is starts to feel far more complicated than anyone imagined.

The greatest threat isn't always outside the walls. A political coup drags the Survey Corps into a battle for the capital itself, unearthing a conspiracy the government has buried for a hundred years. Then comes the return to Shiganshina — and a secret in Eren's basement that rewrites everything.

The world beyond the sea is nothing like Paradis imagined — and neither is Eren. As old allies become enemies across the battlefields of Marley, one choice sets an unstoppable force in motion. There is no going back, and no one walks away from this ending unchanged.
